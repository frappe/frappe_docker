The scenario

Al-Noor Academy, a private school group in Riyadh, is opening its third campus in January. You met two of their people at the Riyadh Education Expo in August.

Fatima Al-Rashid (Procurement Manager) wants branded welcome kits for the new campus — shirts and mugs for students, laptops for teaching staff. That deal you will win.

Omar Haddad (Facilities Lead) wants a bulk smartphone order for campus security. That deal you will lose, on budget — because a funnel with only wins proves nothing.

How to run it

  1. Open the ERP in a second window. Every step has a link that jumps straight to the right screen.
  2. Work top to bottom. Stages depend on each other — statuses propagate upward through the chain, so skipping ahead breaks the later checks.
  3. Field values are click-to-copy. Anything not listed, leave at its default.
  4. Tick the green Expect box only when you have actually seen the stated result. That box is the test, not the action above it.
  5. Progress saves in this browser. Stage 10 tells you how to clean up.
